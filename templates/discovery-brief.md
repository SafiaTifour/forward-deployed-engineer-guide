# Discovery brief template

## User
- Who is using the system?
- What role do they play?
- What are they trying to do?

## Workflow
- What is the current workflow?
- Where are the bottlenecks or friction points?
- What is the desired future workflow?

## Baseline
- What is done today without the system?
- What is currently slow, repetitive, or error-prone?

## Success
- What does a successful outcome look like?
- What counts as a poor or risky outcome?

## Constraints
- permissions
- policy rules
- allowed actions
- operating boundaries
- review requirements

## Data
- documents or records used
- ownership and freshness
- source versioning and update schedule

## Non-goals
- tasks explicitly excluded from the system
- unsupported actions
- unsupported assumptions

## Owner
- technical owner
- policy owner
- review owner
- escalation contact

## Example
A support agent needs help drafting grounded policy responses from approved internal documents. The system may suggest a draft and cite sources, but it cannot issue refunds or modify accounts.
