# Rollout and handoff

## Scope
Use the assistant on a limited, approved set of support tasks with human review.

## Owner
- engineering team: technical implementation
- support operations: policy and workflow ownership
- compliance or legal: review of risk and scope

## Monitoring
Track review rate, escalation rate, policy citation failures, and unauthorized-access attempts.

## Escalation
Escalate whenever the answer is uncertain, the data is missing, or the policy is conflicting.

## Fallback
If the system fails, revert to manual drafting based on the existing process.

## Rollback
Disable the assistant or restrict it to a smaller, safer subset of requests if operational quality falls below the accepted threshold.

## Support
Document support procedures and maintain a clear owner for operational incidents.
